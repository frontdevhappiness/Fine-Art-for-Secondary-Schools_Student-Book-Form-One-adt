Copyright is a type of intellectual property that protects original works of authorship as soon as an author fixes the work in a tangible form of expression. Tanzania’s Copyright and Neighbouring Rights Act of 1999 makes it illegal for anyone to use protected work without the author’s permission during the author’s lifetime plus 50 years. In Tanzania, copyright issues are taken care by the Copyright Society of Tanzania (COSOTA).

Political functions

Some forms of art are used to raise awareness on political issues. They also promote democracy and liberation. Banners, posters and monuments for political leaders are good examples of how fine art is used in politics. In performing art; music, poetry, and drama serve the purpose of delivering the intended messages such as encouraging positive changes and development as well as promoting peace based on the current political situations.

Social functions

Is concerned with social life of different people. It expresses ideas and emotions in different ways. For example, in various societies, art is a means of transferring knowledge, transmitting customs, morals and religious values and advocating good manners and proper social values to both elders and the young. Moreover, art has been used in many societies to express emotions and appreciation.

Cultural functions

Artworks can be used to influence cultural and traditional values, promote self-esteem, entertain, warn and educate people on morals and customs. Moreover, artworks can be used to inspire socio-economic changes through activities such as art exhibition, traditional dances and festivals, worshipping ceremonies, drama, paintings and songs. To a great extent, these works of art are important for enhancing cultural development.

Aesthetic enjoyment functions

When someone looks at a beautiful artwork, it might be enjoyable for them. Art enhances aesthetic satisfaction and enlivens our lives with beauty and creativity by stimulating our senses, evoking emotions, and presenting us with new perspectives.
