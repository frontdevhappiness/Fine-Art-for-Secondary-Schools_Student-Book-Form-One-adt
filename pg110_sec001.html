Set up your printing area: Lay down old newspapers or cardboard to protect your work surface from ink spills.

Setup and registration: The screen is mounted onto the screen-printing press, which holds it securely in place.  Proper registration is crucial, as it ensures that each colour layer aligns perfectly with the others in the final print.

Ink application: A specific ink is applied to its respective screen.  A squeegee is used to spread the ink over the open areas of the screen, pushing the ink through the mesh and onto the printing surface.

Printing process: The printer pulls the squeegee across the screen, pushing the ink through the mesh and onto the material being printed.  This process is repeated for each colour layer, allowing the ink to overlap and create the full-colour design.

Curing: Once the printing is complete, the printed material is typically passed through a curing unit or oven.  This process cures the ink, making it more durable and resistant to fading or washing out.

Clean-up: After printing, the screen needs to be thoroughly cleaned to remove any remaining ink and emulsion, allowing it to be reused for future printing works.

Prepare your design: Create or choose a design for your screen printing.  You can draw it directly on a piece of paper or print it from a computer.  For beginners, simple designs with bold lines work best.

Set up your printing area: Lay down old newspapers or cardboard to protect your work surface from ink spills.

Apply ink to the screen: Place your mesh screen over the printing area with the mesh side down.  Pour a little ink along the top edge of the screen.

Spread the ink: Using the squeegee, spread the ink evenly over the design area, applying gentle pressure.  Ensure the entire design is covered with a thin layer of ink.

Print your design: Carefully lift the mesh screen from the printing surface to reveal your printed design.  Allow the print to dry completely before moving on to the next print.

Clean the screen: After printing, clean the ink off the mesh screen using water and a gentle brush.  Make sure the screen is completely dry before storing it for future use.
