Informal balance

Informal balance is also known as asymmetrical balance. It is a process of arranging two unlike objects in a work of art to create equal visual weight. Figure 1.16 shows an example of informal balance.

Radial balance

Radial balance occurs when the elements of design seem to radiate out from the centre of a design. A good example of radial balance can be seen on flower petals, tree rings, a half-cut orange and a bicycle wheel. Figure 1.17 shows the radial balance of motifs.
