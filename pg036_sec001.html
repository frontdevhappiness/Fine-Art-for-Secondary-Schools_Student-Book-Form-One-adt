Relationship between fine art and other disciplines

Fine art is not an isolated subject, it has important connections to many other disciplines and helps to advance the field of education.

It enhances a comprehensive understanding of content in most books of other disciplines.

This is possible due to relevant illustrations used to present complex content as works of fine art.

The following information provides a significant relationship between fine art and other subjects.

  • (a)
    In history, language and archaeology, fine art is a good source of information. It is used to provide reference of objects related to cultural objects of different cultures discussed in those disciplines.
  • (b)
    In music and theatre arts, fine art helps in designing stage, Decorations and performance items like backdrops. It also helps in traditional musical instruments and costumes.
  • (c)
    In visual arts, it improves the learner’s artistic abilities and enables them to make careers. They can win various paying job opportunities like painters, cartoonists, illustrators and graphic artists; hence earn a decent living.
