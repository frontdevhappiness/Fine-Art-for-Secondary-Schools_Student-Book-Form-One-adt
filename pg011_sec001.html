Design

Design is another form of art. It is defined as an outline, a sketch, or a visual idea of a work of art to be created. Works of art such as textile designs, posters, emblems, logos and banners are known as products of design. This is because they appear as mere sketches or outlines throughout the process of creation; until they are produced as complete works of art. Design also refers to the creative activity of planning and organising elements of art, such as lines, shapes, colours, textures and space, in a work of art.
