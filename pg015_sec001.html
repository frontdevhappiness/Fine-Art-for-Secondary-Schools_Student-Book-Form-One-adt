Fine art manifestations

Fine art manifestation is an expression that describes how art can be seen from nature. Fine art manifests itself from two sources which are nature and human activities. Fine art manifestations from nature can be observed in our surrounding environment and through all living organisms such as plants, insects, animals, and human beings. This shows that art can freely manifest itself in nature independent from human activities. Examples of fine art manifestation in nature can be seen in various environmental phenomena such as sunrise and sunset, rainbow and physical features such as landscapes. Fine art manifestations in nature can be expressed in plants, insects and animals.

Fine art manifestation in plants: Fine art manifests itself in plants in different ways. For instance, some plants are rich in colours; other plants have attractive fruits and leaves. Examples of plants with colourful flowers include roses, perennial bulb plants and sunflower. Plants like apple, mango and grape trees bear beautiful fruits. Other plants display beauty through their leaves. These include the coleus plant which has colourful leaves and aglaonema which has spotted leaves.
