Each of the examples provided in this section is important in showing the significance of visuals as used by artists to express daily life experiences. The stories and moments in the visual arts illustrate beauty, scenery, and different chores by the people in Tanzania. Thus, through visual arts people learn about the importance of preserving our local culture and environment.
