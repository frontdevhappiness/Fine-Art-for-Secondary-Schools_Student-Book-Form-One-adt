Fine Art for Secondary Schools: Student’s Book Form One

Rainbow across the sky: A rainbow is an attractive multi-coloured curve in the sky. It is made when the sunlight strikes water droplets. The sunlight is scattered by water droplets to formulate a huge colourful arch with seven colours in the following order: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo and violet (ROYGBIV). Figure 1.12 shows a rainbow image.

Art manifestation through physical features of landscape: Landscapes are visible features found on land. They include mountains, hills, water bodies, waterfalls and plants. These features attract our attention with their beauty. Landscape features show the natural beauty of the earth. For example, the beauty of mount Kilimanjaro attracts many tourists from different parts of the world to visit Tanzania and enjoy it. Water bodies such as lakes Victoria, Nyasa, Tanganyika and other natural forms create excitement when they are looked at.
