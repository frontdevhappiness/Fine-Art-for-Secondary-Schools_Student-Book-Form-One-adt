Steps in drawing

Drawing is an activity that is organised in a series of stages or steps. There are four main steps to be followed in drawing. These are: sketching, developing a sketch, shading, error correction and refinement or completion.

Sketching

This is the first step which involves outlining the shape and form of an object by using light pencil lines that can be easily created. When sketching an object, it is important to consider the basics of sketching such as drawing primary lines and shapes. Figure 2.19 shows an example of a sketch.
