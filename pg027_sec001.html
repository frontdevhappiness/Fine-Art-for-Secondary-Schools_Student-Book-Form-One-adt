Principle of unity

The principle of unity unifies all other principles within a work of art, allowing each element to coexist with one another to form a beautiful composition. It conveys completeness and wholeness in a work of art.

Elements of fine art

These are sometimes known as elements of design or elements of art. In fine art, communication is done through symbols or signs. Different societies use symbols to express ideas, and feelings in their works of art. Visual elements of art are made of basic signs or symbols that artists use in their works of art to convey messages to the audience or viewers. The visual elements of art are line, shape, form, space, value, colour and texture. These elements are explained as follows:

Line

This is a basic element of art which is defined as a moving point in space. When two or more points are joined, a line is formed. A line is a fundamental element of art because it is applied in the formation of other elements such as shapes, forms and texture. The major types of lines are vertical lines, horizontal lines, diagonal lines, zigzag lines and curved lines.
