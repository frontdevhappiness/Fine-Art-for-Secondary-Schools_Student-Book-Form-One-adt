Painting

This is a form of fine art in which ideas, thoughts, emotions, and feelings are creatively expressed by using coloured images. Painting is a work of art made from paints. Painting can be made on walls, paper and canvas by using brushes and colours. A good choice and effective application of colours in painting create a skilful and complete composition. In addition, the skilful use of colours adds aesthetic value to paintings in particular and the general surroundings in which they are displayed. Figure 1.2 shows an example of a painting.

Sculpture

This is a three-dimensional art form that involves the creation of physical objects or forms by manipulating and shaping materials. Sculptures can be produced by using a variety of media or materials. These include clay, wax, wood, paper, stone, metal, fabric, rubber, glass, gypsum powder and cement. Sculpture can be figurative or non-figurative work of art. A complete sculpture can be in relief; showing three dimensions of the artwork, in intaglio and free-standing or sculpture in the round.
