Trends of art in Tanzania

Introduction

Art in Tanzania encompasses various forms of artistic expression reflecting the country’s rich history, cultural, social, political, and economic status.  The art production ranges from simple to complex; that is from self-taught creative artists to skilled professionals, and includes a wide variety of genres.  In this chapter, you will learn about art in Tanzania, the Tingatinga painting movement and the Makonde sculpture movement.  The competencies developed will enable you to produce various artworks which then make you an entrepreneur in the world of art.

Concept of trends of art in Tanzania

Trends of art simply mean “tendencies in art” typically used to depict artistic latest development in terms of form, subject, style or any other aesthetic dimensions.  Tanzania, like other countries all over the world has experienced such tendencies all the time.

Between rock art painting and modern times, Tanzanian art has been evolving in styles.  Rock painting emerged through the ancient rock painters who lived during the Stone Age however, they managed to express their feelings and memories, through painting their life experiences which involved hunting and gathering.  The oldest paintings made during the Stone Age are found in the Kondoa Irangi rock shelters in the central regions of Tanzania (See Figure 5.1).  Similar rock paintings were later discovered in several rock shelters surrounding Lake Victoria in the Mara and Mwanza regions.

Some of the main features of prehistoric rock paintings were the use of various geometrical patterns, and figures of fauna and flora painted on rock surfaces.
