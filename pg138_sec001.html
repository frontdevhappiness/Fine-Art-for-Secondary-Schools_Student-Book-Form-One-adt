Current situation of art in Tanzania

The trends of art in Tanzania were not a limited point of creativity to Tanzanian artists. They were like a road map of cultural entities to grow. During the colonial period, several talented Africans were formally trained to become professional painters and art teachers in various schools in Tanzania and at the Makerere University College in Uganda. Abdullah Farahani, Sam Ntiro, Fatma Abdullah, and professor Elias Jengo, and Raza Mohamed are among a few Tanzanian painters who acquired formal training in visual art.
