Sculpture technique

This is a method of creating sculpture which involves various techniques.

Sculptures can be produced by using various materials including wood, stone, cement, gypsum powder, rubber and sponge just to mention a few.

The materials to be used should be solid enough to hold a form even when some parts have been removed.

There are two ways of making sculpture which are subtractive and additive.

The subtractive method is used to take away some parts from the material to get the required form.

The additive method refers to adding up some materials until to get the required form.

Sculpture can be done in two techniques which are relief sculpture and sculpture in the round as discussed below.

Relief sculpture

This is a method in which the image is carved and bonded to a solid background of the same material.

Creating sculpture in relief is to give the impression that the carved item area has been raised above the background plane.
