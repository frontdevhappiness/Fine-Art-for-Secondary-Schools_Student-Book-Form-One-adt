Transforming waste into artwork

One of the significances of waste is that it can be used to create other things valuable like artwork.

Materials needed

Assorted waste materials (For example, damaged paper, carving remnants, excess paint, discarded plastic, metal scraps), scissors, glue, protective gloves, aprons, paintbrushes, markers, art tools, recycled canvas or cardboard as the base for the artworks, and any other related materials.

Instructions

Material gathering: Materials can be gathered in advance from a variety of places, including recycling facilities, homes, and creative studios. The primary factors to take into account are the materials’ textures, forms, colours and the use of an intended artwork.

Theme or message: The selection of a theme for the artwork should be based on the art teacher’s guidance.

Produce artwork: Students creativity should be guided by the art teachers. Present and discuss the artwork: This should take into account the experiences, difficulties, and potential solutions that were encountered throughout the entire process.

Activity 6.3

Collect various waste materials found around your fine art class. Then assemble them to create artwork.
