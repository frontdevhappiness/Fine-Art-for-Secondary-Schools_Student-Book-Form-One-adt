Intaglio printing process

Intaglio printing is a traditional printing process used to produce high-quality images or designs on paper, currency, postage stamps, and other valuable documents.  The term “intaglio” comes from the Italian word for “engraved” or “cut in,” which accurately describes the key characteristic of this printing method.  The intaglio printing process is known for its ability to produce fine details, rich tones, and high-quality prints, making it particularly suitable for printing banknotes, security documents, and valuable art prints.  The complexity and craftsmanship involved in intaglio printing also make it difficult to copy.  This has contributed to its use in anti-counterfeiting measures for currency and other important documents.  Figure 4.4 shows the Intaglio block motif with its result after being stamped on the surface.
