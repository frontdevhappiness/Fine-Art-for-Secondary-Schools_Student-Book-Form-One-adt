Procedures for using natural colours from the surroundings to paint

The following are procedures for using natural colours from the surroundings to paint:

Identify sources of natural colours: Look around your school environment, or go for a nature walk to find colourful materials like flowers, leaves, fruits and roots that can be used to make paints.

Gather the materials: Pick a few fallen leaves, flowers, fruits and roots. It is advised that you should not harm living plants or the environment.

Drying and crushing the materials: After gathering the materials, allow them to air dry for one or two days. Once they are dry, crush and grind them properly into fine granules.

Add a binder: Mix a little bit of water with the crushed pigment to make a paste. This paste helps the colour stick to our paper or canvas.

Test the colours: Dip your paintbrush into the natural paint and try it on a piece of paper. Observe how beautiful the colours are.

Create different colours: To make different colours, try to mix different pigments. For example, mix red and blue pigments to get purple.

Paint a picture: Now that you have your natural paint, use it to paint various pictures.

Storing the paint: If you have any remaining paints, you can keep them in small containers with lids. Make sure to label the colours on each container.
