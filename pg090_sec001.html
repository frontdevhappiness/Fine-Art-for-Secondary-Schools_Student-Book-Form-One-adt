Material needed for extracting colours from soil

The following are materials needed for extracting colours from soil:

  • (a) Soil with different colours;
  • (b) Small bowls or containers (one for each colour) that you don’t use in the kitchen;
  • (c) Glue (preferably wood glue);
  • (d) Wooden spoon for stirring;
  • (e) Painting brushes, and
  • (f) Painting papers ideally watercolour.

Procedures for extracting colours from soil

The following are the procedures for extracting colours from soil:

Collect soil of different colours: Collect soil of different colours from different places and separate it into bowls based on the colours.

Soil preparation: Remove all unnecessary particles, allow the soil to dry and crush it to get a powder form.

Mixing process: Pour the powder into the bowls, add water and glue and mix it well to get a smooth mixture. It is now ready for use.

Painting procedure of using colours from soil

Use watercolour brushes: Use brushes to paint the same way as you can do for colours from flowers and leaves. Apply several layers of the painting one after the other and allow them to dry.

Leave for dry and display: The painted artwork should be given time to dry, before it is displayed for class discussion.

Creating colours from soil can be a fun and educational activity. This activity will solve challenges of material availability but also add skills that can trigger more creative ideas.
