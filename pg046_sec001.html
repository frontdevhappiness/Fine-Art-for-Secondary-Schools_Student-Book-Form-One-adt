Marker pens:

These are ink-based sketching, writing or drawing tools. They have a container filled with ink and are made in different forms. Some marker pens have a broad tip while others are dual-brush pens with a fine tip and some have a chisel tip. Marker pens can be applied on different surfaces such as paper, wall, iron or concrete. Chalk:

These are normally used in drawing on surfaces such as walls, boards, wood and on other objects which a drawing can be done. Chalk contains small particles of dust which are well seen when used and make it suitable for marking temporary streaks on a surface. Charcoal is found in different shapes, sizes and colours.
