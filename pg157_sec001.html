Radialtype of balance arranged or having parts arranged like rays
Reflectionan action or process of light, heat, or sound being thrown back from a surface
Rhythma strong, regular repeated pattern of movement or sound
Scenerythe natural features of a landscape considered in terms of their appearance
Sculptoran artist who makes sculptures
Semi-circlehalf of a circle or its circumference
Significancequality of being worthy of attention; importance
Sketchinga rough or unfinished drawing or painting, often made to assist in making a more finished picture
Spectruma band of colours, as seen in a rainbow, produced by the separation of the components of light by their different degrees of refraction according to wavelength
Still lifea drawing or painting that focuses on still or inanimate objects
Strokea mark made by drawing a pen, pencil, or paintbrush in one direction across paper or canvas
Support basea structure that physically holds the painting, drawing or a paint film, for example, canvas, plywood, metal, paper, and boards
Syntheticpaint made with synthetically manufactured resins rather than with naturally occurring oils or gums
Texturethe quality of something that can be decided by touch; the degree to which something is rough or smooth
Three dimension (3-D)an appearance that features length, breadth, and depth
Tonequality of brightness, deepness, or hue of a shade of a colour
Turpentinea volatile strong oil distilled from gum turpentine or pine wood, used in mixing paints and varnishes and in liniment
