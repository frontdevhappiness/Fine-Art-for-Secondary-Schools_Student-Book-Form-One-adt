Concept of painting

Painting is a form of fine art in which ideas, thoughts, emotions, and feelings are creatively expressed by using images in colours. The work of art made from painting is also called a painting. Painting can be made by using brushes and colours on surfaces such as walls, paper and canvas. Painting is among the oldest and most famous forms of fine art. Painting has always been practised alongside drawing, design and sculpture.

Painting materials

In fine art, the materials for painting a work of art are known as media. In painting, the term media or medium means paints or colours that are used by artists when painting. Paints are made of three components, namely pigment, binder and solvent. The pigment is a fine ground, a coloured powder that gives paint its colour or hue.
