Occupational health and environmental care

Introduction

The development of industry and technology contributes significantly to environmental problem. Our daily life activities have also become a source of generating waste in our homes. Waste has caused serious harm to our lives including diseases, injuries and environmental pollution. If we need to live a healthy life, waste management should be the responsibility of each of us starting from the level of the individual, family, institution and the country as a whole. Schools are also among the sources of waste production in the workplace. The fine arts industry also contributes to the generation of waste through its various activities. In this chapter, you will learn the concept of waste, the types of waste and different ways to dispose it. The skills developed will enable you to protect yourself and your colleagues from injury when using various techniques, materials and tools of the fine arts. Not only that but you will also protect the environment as you will have the skills and knowledge of waste disposal.

Waste management for social well-being of artist

Concept of occupational health and environmental care

Occupational health and environmental care are two distinct fields that share a common goal of ensuring the social well-being of the people in the workplace. Both fields are essential in promoting public health by ensuring the safety and prevention of human beings from environmental hazards. The goal is to minimise the impact of waste materials on the environment by reducing the amount of waste generated due to the development of industry, technology and our daily life activities.

Concept of waste

Waste is a concept that is defined as materials that are unwanted by their producer. These materials have to be managed to make everyone in particular environment
