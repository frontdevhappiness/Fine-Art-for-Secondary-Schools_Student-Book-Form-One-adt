Activity 2.9

Use steps of drawing by using ordinary charcoal to draw a simple still-life image of an orange, a banana, and a pawpaw placed on a plate. Then, apply the smudging technique to create form.

Revision Exercise

1. Emphasis is purposely created to attract the viewer’s attention. Describe how this principle is achieved.

2. There are two types of texture – natural and visual. How do the two textures differ from each other.

3. There are different ways and equipment used to preserve a drawing. Explain how you will use fixative spray for this function.

4. Match the shading techniques in List A with their corresponding descriptions in List B.

List A List B
(i) Hatching
(ii) Dotting
(iii) Smudging
(iv) Cross-hatching
(v) Scribbling
  • A. Applying lines roughly in a circular movement to create a shade.
  • B. Drawing lines across each other.
  • C. Changing the value by adjusting the amount of pressure applied to the medium or using blending tools.
  • D. Applying lines towards the same direction.
  • E. Creating values by applying dots to the picture.
  • F. Applying smearing movement on a drawn object to indicate the source of light.
  • G. Applying dots and line together to create light and dark tone.
