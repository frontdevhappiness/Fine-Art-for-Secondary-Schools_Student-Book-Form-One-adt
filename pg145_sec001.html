Types of waste from fine art activities

Fine art wastes falls under industrial waste. There are various types of activities in fine art which generate waste. Some of these include carvings, drawing, painting, and printmaking. In these activities, different wastes are produced including oily rags wastes, solvent wastes, paint wastes, baby oil wastes, linseed oil wastes, pencil leftover wastes, sharp implements wastes and empty containers wastes.
