Introduction

Printmaking is part and parcel of our life. It offers huge value to our daily life as it allows images and texts to be reproduced. Every sector of our life is full of printmaking. The clothes we wear are mostly products of printmaking, our books and every business advertisement we see all over hanging in streets in the form of posters and billboards are made possible because of printmaking. It helps us to feel comfortable and enjoy life. In this chapter, you will learn the concept of printmaking with focus on meaning, techniques, materials, tools and equipment used in printmaking and creating simple decorative monoprints. The competencies developed will enable you to create and produce simple prints which will then make you an entrepreneur in printmaking activities.

Concept of printmaking

Printmaking is an artistic process based on the principle of transferring images from a matrix onto another surface, most often paper or fabric. A print is an artwork made up of ink on a flat surface such as canvas, paper, fabric and cups. The plate is where you transfer and create the original image to print multiple copies. The process is not created by painting or drawing images on a surface, but pressing the paper to an inked surface of the image. Printmaking is developed to help artists get more copies of the original artwork within a few times. For example, an artist can manage to print 500 T-shirts for a particular campaign within a day through the printmaking process. Such a task could take longer if other methods like painting or drawing are used. Printmaking is also used to make rubber stamps, print on complex areas like high-height walls and decorate fabrics like table covers, curtains and bed sheets. Printmaking is done in different ways and techniques.
