Commercial advertisement

Drawing is regularly used in commercial products. On many goods packages, there are always illustrations drawn on them. Drawings have been used to beautify the packages in addition to advertising them. Drawings are also used by architects, engineers, and book illustrators to clarify complex concepts, thus making them a viable source of income for the artists who engage in drawing. Decorative images

Many items are made more attractive by using sketched graphics to embellish them. The decorations are drawn on objects using a variety of symbols and images.
