Significance of drawing in our life

From various perspectives, drawing has significant roles in our lives. Some of these functions of drawing are creation of directions, commercial advertisements, decorative images and expression of personal feelings as discussed below.

Direction signs

We frequently come across numerous illustrations used as signage in a variety of locations. They are frequently drawn on purpose to give directions to users of various locations, roads, streets, and different structures.
