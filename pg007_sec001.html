Introduction to Fine Art

Introduction

Fine art plays a great role in our daily activities and touches every aspect of our lives in many ways. The images you see in various books, the emblem printed on your uniform, and the decorations you perceive at various places are some examples of fine art products. It is also an important aspect of our culture. Different societies in the world have been making and using fine art products for different purposes. These include storytelling, ceremonial occasion and prestige. In this chapter, you will learn the concept of fine art, its principles, elements of fine art, and its relationships with other disciplines. The competencies developed will enable you to appreciate fine art and later on become an entrepreneur in creating and selling artwork.

Concept of fine art

To better understand the concept of fine art, let us have the general meaning of art and its various forms.

Art refers to the general term comprising human creativity and imagination to produce various art forms. For many years, people have been using different art forms to express feelings, ideas, emotions and thoughts. Additionally, art has been regarded as the highest form of expression in human life. Through various art forms, artists create works of art that are used as an alternative communication to deliver the desired messages. A form of art is made of all external or physical characteristics of an artwork. It also depends on the size and type of materials or media used in the creation of a work of art. Artists use different art forms to express their imagination and give tangible or visual structures to their creative ideas.
