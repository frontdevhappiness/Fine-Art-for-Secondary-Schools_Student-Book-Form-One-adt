Functions of fine art in human life

Throughout the history of human civilisation, people have been using different forms of art as means to communicate various ideas and express their feelings. Similarly, Fine art plays different important roles in our life. These include educational, historical and identity preservation, economic, political, social, religious, and cultural roles. It is also source of enjoyment in our life. These functions can be elaborated as follows:

Education functions

Fine art has the power to improve understanding and change people’s behaviour through the incorporation of educational themes into works of art. The art can be used in different contexts, such as making teaching aids like, book illustrations, map, and caricature drawing. It is also used in campaigning materials like posters and banners, because it fosters understanding and creativity; improves the ability to analyse; and provides a platform for self-expression.

History and identity preservation

Drawing, painting, printmaking, and sculpture, frequently contain images reflecting the history, culture, and identity of a particular society. They are passed down to new generations through art, which helps maintain a sense of oneness and belonging among members of a particular cultural group.

Economic functions

Artists earn income by selling artworks or exhibiting on different platforms such as in local art galleries, museums and online markets. They may also be commissioned to produce artwork for various events.
