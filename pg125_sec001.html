Technique, materials and equipment for producing Tingatinga paintings

Creating a Tingatinga painting involves a specific and colourful style of art that originated in Tanzania. The following are the materials needed to create a Tingatinga painting:

Materials needed

Canvas or sturdy paper, acrylic paints (bright colours), painting brushes (flat and round), black acrylic paint, water for rinsing brushes, a palette or a smooth surface for mixing colours.

Instructions

(a)
Introduce Tingatinga Art: Observe various examples of Tingatinga paintings, and find their origin and distinct characteristics. Highlight the use of bold colours, black outlines, and stylised patterns.
(b)
Choose subject: Choose wildlife subjects such as elephants, giraffes, lions, zebras, etc. pick your favourite animal as the subject for your Tingatinga style painting.
(c)
Preparation of the painting surface: Use emulsion to paint the surface of the canvas to smoothen it. Leave it to dry for some hours to prevent painted colours from overlapping one another.
(d)
Paint the background colours: Use a palette to prepare the vibrant hues, then paint the whole canvas surface. Wait for some hours to dry.
(e)
Sketch the animal: Use a pencil or fine-pointed painting brush to sketch a composition of your chosen animal figure on the dry emulsion-painted surface of your canvas focusing on its basic shapes and features.
(f)
Colour Choice: Make colour choice and preparation according to your preference for the types of motif designs that you will use on the objects.
