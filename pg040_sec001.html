Introduction

Drawing is a basic skill which should be acquired before learning other skills in fine art. In that sense, anybody with good drawing knowledge and skills is capable of working with all other forms of visual arts such as painting and printmaking. In this chapter, you will learn the concept of drawing, materials and equipment for drawing, handling materials and equipment, shading techniques, steps in drawing, creating simple decorative drawings and traditional techniques in drawing. The competencies developed will enable you to create drawings for different purposes by using various techniques. The skills developed will later on enable you to become an entrepreneur in creating artworks.

Drawn images and their expressions

Concept of drawing

Drawing is a form of fine art in which artists create images by using lines. Drawing can be made to mark or represent shapes of objects on the surface of a paper or a piece of wood, board or canvas. Drawing is the oldest form of art. In the past, some societies used drawing to communicate and preserve information about their history and life experiences. Recently, drawing has become a very popular means of artistic expression among members of different societies. Drawing is the act of making marks on a surface. It is the basic language of art and a direct tool for communication and expression of ideas. Drawing also serves to describe, record, and challenge what can be observed around us. When we draw, we are at work, engaging physically with our senses and minds.

Traditionally, drawing is done by free hand technique. Development in science and technology has brought many changes in drawing art practices.
