Fine Art for Secondary Schools: Student’s Book Form One

Glaze
a glasslike substance coated onto the surface of a pottery item that gives a glossy clear finish
Gum Arabic or Arabic gum
water-soluble powdered natural gum material used as an additive to glaze, a binder or suspending agent
Graphite
a soft grey crystalline composed of carbon used in lead pencils and a solid lubricant
Gothic
a popular typeface style in the Middle Ages from 1200-1500. The term originated from the Italians who used it to refer to the “barbaric” letter forms of the Black letter
Horizon
the line at which the earth’s surface and the sky appear to meet
Illustration
visualisation created by an artist
Imagination
the ability of the mind to be creative or resourceful
Linseed oil
a pale-yellow oil extracted from linseed, used especially in paint and varnish
Medium
an agency or means of doing something
Nib
a part of a quill, dip pen, fountain pen, or stylus which comes into contact with the writing surface to deposit ink
Paint
a coloured substance which is spread over a surface and dries to leave a thin decorative or protective coating
A palette
a thin board or slab on which an artist lays and mixes colours
Pastel
a crayon made of powdered pigments bound with gum or resin
Pattern
a repeated decorative design
Pigment
a substance that gives something a particular colour when it is present in it or is added to it
A portrait
a painting, drawing, photograph, or engraving of a person, especially one depicting only the face or head and shoulders
Prism
a transparent optical element with flat, polished surfaces that are designed to refract light
