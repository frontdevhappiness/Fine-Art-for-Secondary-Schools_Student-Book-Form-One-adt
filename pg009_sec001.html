Fine artists use their creativity, skills and personal perceptions to communicate their own or other people’s emotions, ideas, and observations about the world. Fine art is a potential means of expression for artists to comment on personal, cultural, social and political issues.

Fine artworks can be found in various places including residential areas, art shops, galleries, public spaces, museums, individual collections and online sources, for example, https://www.artfinder.com.

Forms of fine art

Fine arts cover a wide range of artistic skills that are created and appreciated for their beauty and expressive power. With the advancement of technology, artists are exposed to various techniques and media to create tangible and visual ideas. This development extends several fine art forms which include drawing, painting, design, printmaking and sculpture. These forms are further explained as follows:

Drawing

This is a process of creating images on a surface using a marking tool such as a pencil, charcoal or crayon. Drawing is a basic step when developing other forms of fine art including painting and designing. A drawing can also be used as an independent work of art to portray a message to an audience.
