Forms of art

There are three main forms of art namely visual, literary, and performing arts. These forms are explained as follows:

Visual arts

These are creative works of art that are appreciated or enjoyed by eyesight. Examples of visual arts are drawing, painting, sculpture, printmaking, design, craftworks, photography, filmmaking and architecture. These works of art are created to express the artist’s ideas and imagination in visual forms. Visual arts are among the major tools for teaching and learning in many ways. They include the use of illustration in books, digital presentations and other teaching aids such as spheres, toys, periodicals and photographs. Applying visual arts skills and knowledge makes learning enjoyable because it helps students relate classroom activities to outdoor activities such as domestic chores.

Literary arts

The term literary arts refer to a form of art which is delivered in a spoken or written form. Literary arts include poems, plays, stories, novels as well as other creative works which are closely related to spoken or written communication. Works of literary art include magazines, letters, manuscripts, and books. Literary arts have different genres such as drama, poetry, and prose. Each genre distinguishes itself in the essence of how it presents messages.

Performing arts

Performing arts is a form of art in which a range of artistic activities are presented on the stage before an audience. These kinds of arts include acrobatics, music and theatre which are performed through body balance, vocals and instruments as well as life experiences respectively.

Meaning of fine art

Fine art refers to forms of visual arts that are primarily produced and valued for their aesthetic, utilitarian and intellectual significance. The origin of fine art is human activities involved in creative processes. These processes involve preparations, incubation or putting together production ideas and materials, composition of a work of art, evaluation of the work of art, and production or completion of a work of art.
