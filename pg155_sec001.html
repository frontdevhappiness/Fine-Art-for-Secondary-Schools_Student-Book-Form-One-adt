Glossary

Aesthetica taste, pleasure or appreciation of the value and beauty of art
Architecturethe art or practice of designing and constructing buildings
Beeswaxa product extracted or made from honeycombs that is used to make wood polishes and candles
Bristlesthick hair or hair-like pieces of plastic fibre which are attached to a brush
Calligraphyan artistic writing style that usually involves a nib pen or brush where the pressure is varied to create thick and thin lines, all in a single stroke.
Canvasa strong, heavy cloth made of cotton, hemp, or linen used as a surface for oil painting
Chisel tipmarkers have a slanted end that allows for both thicker and thinner lines
Competenciesthe ability to do something successfully or efficiently
Concreteconstruction material composed of fine and coarse aggregates bound together with cement
Crayona stick of pigmented wax used for writing or drawing on paper
Enamela paint that dries to give a smooth or hard coat
Equilibriuma state in which a process and its reverse are occurring at equal rates so that no overall change is taking place
Ferrulea metal ring used to strengthen or seal an attached material to a handle
Fibrea thread or filament from which a vegetable tissue, mineral substance, or textile is formed
Fura short, fine, soft hair of certain animals
Genrea category of art, music, or literature
