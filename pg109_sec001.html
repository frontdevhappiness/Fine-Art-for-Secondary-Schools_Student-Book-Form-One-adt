Steps in creating a mesh screen

The following are steps in creating a mesh screen:

  1. (a)

    Create a frame by using pieces of wood then bind the mesh on it;

  2. (b)

    Mix photo emulsion with a little amount of sensitiser then stir the mixture. This should be done in a dark place;

  3. (c)

    Pick a photo emulsion by using a squeegee then apply it on the mesh screen (in a dark room);

  4. (d)

    Dry the mesh screen using a heat gun, drier or fan and leave it for a moment to let the screen dry;

  5. (e)

    Take a motif on printed paper and attach it to the back side of the mesh screen. The motif should face the mesh screen. The image of the motif should be touching the mesh screen then, pour kerosene on the paper motif in a dark place;

  6. (f)

    Place a transparent mirror on the screen mesh behind the paper motif and tight both the mirror, the paper motif and the screen mesh.

  7. (g)

    Expose the combination of mesh, the motif and the mirror on the required light. Remember, the transparent mirror should face the light from 2 minutes to 10 minutes. But it depends on the amount of source of light and the mixture of photo emulsion;

  8. (h)

    Separate the mirror and the motif paper from the screen, then clean the screen slowly by using a sponge and water. Once a motif is visible on the screen, use the spray gun to wash the screen until the motif is seen.

  9. (i)

    Dry the screen using a heat gun or sunlight.

Note, in case the motif does not appear on the screen mesh, it is likely that the sensitizer is excessive in the photo emulsion or the time of exposing it to light rays has been insufficient. The solution to such a problem is either to add a sensitizer in photo emulsion or increase exposure to light rays.
