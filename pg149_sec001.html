Procedures for collecting waste from fine art activities

The following are the procedures for collecting waste from fine art activities:

(a) Collect all unwanted materials or tools and place them in appropriate labelled waste containers according to their types,

(b) Then, close the containers when they are not in use, and

(c) Send them to dispose when the containers are full.
