Create painting colours obtained from the surrounding

One of the sources for painting supplies is the environment. Natural elements in our environment can give artists access to a variety of natural colours. The natural colours of our environment range from many things such as flowers and leaves, fruits and roots to coloured soils.

Painting colours from flowers and leaves

Look at the vibrant flowers and leaves at your school. They are filled with lovely hues. These flowers and leaves can produce colours for painting. Figure 3.17 shows flowers and leaves in vibrant colours.
